## Full description
Scrappy is a practical scrap-metal companion for identifying common materials, estimating value, comparing yard quotes, and planning loads before you sell.

Use the camera identification workflow to get a visual AI suggestion for common scrap categories, then confirm the actual grade with your receiving scrapyard. Scrappy also includes a metal reference library, motor value comparisons, mixed-load estimates, trip-cost calculations, saved yard quotes, price snapshots, sale records, weather, radar, and tools for exporting or sharing your own estimates and records.

Scrappy can help you compare whole-item value with estimated material recovery, including labor and trip costs. Saved quotes let you compare yards using information you enter and maintain yourself.

Camera identification and value calculations are estimates, not laboratory analysis, guaranteed alloy identification, live purchase offers, or guaranteed payouts. Scrap prices vary by yard, grade, quantity, contamination, preparation, and market conditions. Always confirm classification, acceptance rules, and final price with the receiving scrapyard.

Scrappy does not require GPS location permission. Weather uses the city you choose. Calling, directions, and nearby-yard searches open the appropriate external app on your device.
